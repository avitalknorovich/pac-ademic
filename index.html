PAC-ADEMIC

The campus is a maze: three buildings, five floors each. Floors 1–3 are linked by corridors; floors 4–5 are dead-end branches you can only leave by the stairs.

Eat every dot. Grab a power pellet (most are hidden upstairs) to turn the ghosts blue.

THE GHOSTS

Blinky — chases you along the shortest path. Gets faster near the end.
Pinky — aims 4 tiles ahead of you to cut you off.
Inky, the Gatekeeper — when you go up to floor 4–5 he guards the stairwell on floor 3. Otherwise he blocks your nearest corridor or staircase.
Clyde — chases you from far away, backs off when he gets close.
